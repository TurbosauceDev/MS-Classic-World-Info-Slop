How to use the EXP/hr calculator
  • Before you start, note your EXP from the bar at the bottom of the screen (e.g. 123456[45.67%]) and press Start timer.
  • Train for 5 to 10 minutes, then press Stop and type the new EXP. If you leveled up, raise End level.
  • EXP boxes take the number, the percentage (45.67%) or the whole bar text. Or skip the timer and type the minutes.
  • Pick the map, party and EXP card to compare with our Where to train estimate for that map (default build for your class and level). Our estimate counts no EXP card, so it's multiplied by your card here.
  • Save keeps the session in this browser only. Sort by date or EXP/hr to compare maps.
