How to use the EXP/hr calculator
  • Live (desktop browser, Windows or macOS): pick your class and map, press Share game window and pick the game. The page reads your EXP bar and the pickup lines like the Grind Tracker app does: EXP, mesos and kills per hour, loot value, potions used, time to level. The picture stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
  • Time with no EXP, meso or item for a minute isn't counted, and the clock stops while the EXP bar is off screen (Cash Shop, changing channel). Pause for a break. Stop saves the run below.
  • Mini view (Chrome, Edge) opens a small always-on-top window with the numbers, to keep over the game.
  • The browser can't read your map or class off the screen (the app does), so pick them here. Pickup lines must be on screen (the default chat setting) for mesos, kills and loot.
  • From two screenshots: take one when you start and one when you stop, then drop, pick or paste both into the box. It reads the EXP bar on each and the time each was taken (camera data, else a date in the file name, else the file's time; pasted = the moment you paste).
  • By hand: type your EXP from the bar (e.g. 123456[45.67%], the number or 45.67%), press Start timer, train 5 to 10 minutes, press Stop and type the new EXP. If you leveled up, raise End level.
  • Map, party and EXP card compare your rate with our Where to train estimate for that map (default build for your class and level, × your card). Save keeps the session in this browser only; Save as image makes a picture to share.
  • EXP needed above level 49 is meowdb's historical table (not confirmed in game), so time to level is an estimate there.
