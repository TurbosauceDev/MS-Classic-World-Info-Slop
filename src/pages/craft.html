How crafting works

Getting started

  • Six professions, each taught by a master NPC (Reference tab). From character level 10, finish the master's apprentice quest to learn it at craft Lv 1.
  • Weaponcrafting's apprentice quest needs Smithing's apprentice quest done first, plus a Bronze and an Iron Ingot (Smithing).
  • Professions feed each other: arrows need Smithing ingots, the Red Chair needs Tailoring cloth and Weaponcrafting screws. Click any recipe for every part.
  • Set Your craft levels once: Plan, Recipes ("Only my levels") and Level up all use them.

Leveling and profit

  • Every craft costs a fee and gives craft EXP. Craft levels go 1 to (the launch client's cap; the game files still list some Lv 9-10 recipes, hidden here). Each craft level also needs a character level: 5 × the craft level (Lv 2 at 10, Lv 8 at 40).
  • Kits (weekly "in Need of Help" quests, craft Lv 2+): +10% craft EXP for 15 min. Catalysts (craft Lv 5 quests): a chance to change a weapon's or armor's bonus stats, or to upgrade a Lesser scroll; the odds aren't in the game files.
  • Profit = what the result sells for (the site's usual price: meso.watch median sale, then classicmarket.kr, then meowdb; or what an NPC pays if that's more) minus the fee minus what the ingredients would sell for. A crafted part with no price counts at its own from-scratch cost. Equips are valued as plain ones.
  • Plan: add your inventory (the same Equip, Use, Etc and bank screenshots the Price check reads, or typed in) to see what to craft now, what you're close to, and the best mix.

Where the numbers come from

  • Game files (launch client, OSMS export Oct 17): recipes, craft levels, fees, craft EXP, quests, item stats, NPC prices.
  • OSMS Data Explorer crafting tab: craft EXP per level and the character-level gate (capped at Lv 8; Lv 1-8 unchanged from COT2).
  • Prices: meso.watch (as of ), classicmarket.kr, meowdb; meowdb shop list for NPC shops.
  • Estimates: no drop or success rates exist in the files. Farming times take every reported drop as 5% per kill (this site's assumption) unless grind tracker players shared 100+ kills of that monster; kills per hour use Where to train's model with the default build, alone. Every craft is taken as a success.

Ingredients

Cheapest way to level a profession

For each craft level from yours, the recipe with the lowest cost per craft EXP: its fee, the fees of parts you make on the way, and the raw materials at the price you pick (what you give up by not selling them; an estimate). Materials with no price count as free and are flagged. Crafts needed = craft EXP for that level ÷ the recipe's EXP. Whether low recipes keep giving full EXP at higher craft levels isn't in the game files.
