How crafting works

Getting started

  • Six professions, each taught by a master NPC (cards below). From character level 10, finish the master's apprentice quest to learn it at craft Lv 1.
  • Weaponcrafting's apprentice quest needs Smithing's apprentice quest done first, plus a Bronze and an Iron Ingot (Smithing).
  • Professions feed each other: arrows need Smithing ingots, the Red Chair needs Tailoring cloth and Weaponcrafting screws. The "From scratch" column lists every part.

Leveling a profession

  • Every craft costs mesos and gives craft EXP (columns below). Craft levels go 1 to 8 (the launch client's cap; the game files still list some Lv 9-10 recipes, hidden here).
  • Each craft level also needs a character level: 5 × the craft level (Lv 2 at 10, Lv 8 at 40).
  • Kits (weekly "in Need of Help" quests, craft Lv 2+): +10% craft EXP for 15 min.
  • Catalysts (craft Lv 5 quests): use while crafting for a chance to change a weapon's or armor's bonus stats, or to turn a Lesser scroll into an Intermediate, Greater or Chaos one. The odds aren't in the game files.
  • The six weekly help quests share one pool: 1 is offered per week.

Where the numbers come from

  • Game files (launch client, OSMS export Oct 17): recipes, craft levels, mesos, craft EXP, quests, item stats.
  • OSMS Data Explorer crafting tab: craft EXP per level and the character-level gate (launch client: capped at Lv 8, Lv 1-8 unchanged from COT2).
  • meowdb shop list: dye and helmet prices.
  • Not in the files: drop rates, success rates, and where most ores and Fragment of Magic drop. A "Monster" source is meowdb's grind-maps guide (Leather, Stiff Feather, Animal Fur Bundle, Dragon Skin, Diamond Ore) or a guess from the item's name or description.

Cheapest way to level a profession

For each craft level, the recipe with the lowest cost per craft EXP: its fee plus the fees of parts you make on the way, plus raw materials at what an NPC pays for them (what you give up by not selling them; an estimate). Materials with no NPC price count as free and are flagged. Crafts needed = craft EXP for that level ÷ the recipe's EXP. Whether low recipes keep giving full EXP at higher craft levels isn't in the game files.
