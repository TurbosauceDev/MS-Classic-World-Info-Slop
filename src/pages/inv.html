How the price check works
  • Open your item inventory on the Equip, Use or Etc tab and take a screenshot (Win+Shift+S, PrtSc). One tab per screenshot; scroll and take another if a tab has more than one page. Overlapping screenshots of the same tab are lined up, so nothing is counted twice.
  • Bank: screenshots of the storage window work the same way: scroll down a bit at a time and add each one. When the bank is open, the inventory next to it is left out.
  • Paste (Ctrl+V), drop or pick the screenshots below. Everything is read in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
  • Prices are the median player-shop sale on meso.watch (as of ); half of sales landed in the range shown. Items with no player sales fall back to the NPC price. Prices move, so check before you list.
  • Keep or sell column: the Keep or sell tab's rules with your screenshots' counts. What open quests still want is kept (extras are sold), recipe items are your call, Etc items nothing wants are sold. "Sell now" adds up the sold part at the suggested price.
  • Equips are priced as plain ones: the reader can't see scrolls or stats, so good rolls are worth more.
  • Scroll check: scrolls share one picture per tier, so the screenshot reader can only guess which one you have. "Share game window" reads your screen live instead (Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer): open the Use tab and rest the mouse on each scroll until its slot turns green. The names go straight into the table.
  • Some items share one picture (scrolls of a kind, letters): pick yours from the list in that row. It starts on the one players sell most. A slot that didn't match any item counts as "not read".
