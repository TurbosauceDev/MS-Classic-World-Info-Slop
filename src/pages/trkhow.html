Before you start
  • Install it, open MapleStory, then press Start. It finds the game window by itself.
  • Play at your monitor's native resolution. That's the setup the tracker is built and tested for.
  • Keep the EXP bar, the minimap and the chat box on screen. Kills, mesos and drops come from the chat lines, level and EXP from the EXP bar, the map from the minimap title.
  • For the first few seconds a self-check shows what it can read. A ✗ comes with what to fix and turns ✓ by itself once it works.

A grinding session

  1. Press Start and grind as usual. Breaks over a minute (no EXP, no pickups) are left out of the rates.
  2. Open your inventory on Equip, Use and Etc for a second each after Start. Every visible slot is read, so loot and potion use add up. Scroll each tab to the top.
  3. Put potions and arrows on your quick slots (or keep the Use tab open) so what you use gets counted.
  4. Press Stop, show the three tabs once more, then Finalize log to save it to History with a run summary. Resume carries on instead.

What the numbers mean

  • EXP/hr: EXP per hour of farming. "Last 5 min" is your speed right now.
  • Next level in: time to level at your current EXP/hr.
  • Mesos/hr: mesos picked up. "Net" takes off the potions and arrows you used.
  • Kills/hr: from the "You received EXP" lines, with an estimate when lines scroll by too fast.
  • Loot value/hr: drops at player-shop prices (meso.watch), or the NPC price when there's none.
  • Deaths: EXP bar drops without a level-up, checked against the "You have lost ... EXP" chat line.

Parties

  • Everyone types the same party code in Settings to see each other's EXP/hr, level and job, updated every minute. A name only shows if you type one.
  • Open the in-game party window so the party size is read.
  • The same code makes your party one line on the KPQ leaderboard.

KPQ

  • On Auto, KPQ mode turns on once a KPQ stage shows on the minimap. Pick KPQ in the Session tab to skip the wait in Kerning City too.
  • The session pauses between rounds and picks back up on the next stage. Stage and round times come from the KPQ clock.
  • With sharing on, every round cleared from the 1st stage goes to the KPQ leaderboard. Bonus or Rush is the label you pick.

Sharing

  • Turn it on at first start or in Settings › Share my sessions.
  • Grinding is sent in 15-minute stretches on one map with the same party. A map or party change, or a break over a minute, starts the stretch over.
  • Runs wait on your PC if the server can't be reached and go up later. Turning sharing off drops any still waiting.

Something reads wrong

  • Ctrl+Shift+B (works with the game in front), the Bug button or Settings › Report a bug saves the last ~25 seconds of game pictures and a log to a folder. Your character name shows in those pictures. Send the folder to Danny.
  • Mini shrinks the window to a strip with EXP/hr and time to level. Ctrl + and Ctrl - change the text size.
