What to keep

The list

  • Every item a launch quest (Citizenship included) or a crafting recipe asks for, so you don't vendor it by accident.
  • Verdict: Keep all = nothing known drops or sells it, so you can't replace it. Keep N = what the one-time quests still want, minus what you have. Enough = you have what quests want; sell extras unless you craft. Repeatable = only weekly quests want it. If you craft = only recipes use it.
  • Have: type how many you're holding; it's saved in this browser. Quests you tick done in the Path Planner stop counting.
  • A quest rated Must do or Recommended is marked: keep its items first.
  • Quest items an NPC won't buy (letters, quest-only drops) are left out: you can't vendor them anyway.

Where another comes from

  • Drops: the monster a quest names, monsters players report and confirm on meowdb (votes shown), and meowdb's grind guide. Drop rates aren't known: the server decides them.
  • Shop prices from meowdb's shop list, quest rewards and crafting recipes from the game files.
  • NPC pays is the sell price. players ~ under it is the median player-shop sale price from meso.watch (where it has sales seen); players often pay more for quest and crafting items.
