How Citizenship works

Joining

  • At level 12, sign up in Henesys (Arthur, Town Hall) or Kerning City (Roxy, Civic Center). The intro quest makes you a grade 1 Traveler.
  • Each town has its own grades, quests and shop. Pick a town above to see it.

Earning contribution

  • Daily board: meet each resident once, then their "Asking After" quest repeats daily. Contribution per daily goes up with your grade, not the quest.
  • Weekly donation: bring 100 of one monster drop. Higher grades unlock bigger donations worth more.
  • Story quests: one-time quests that open at grade 2 and 4.

Ranking up

  • Each grade needs a minimum level and a total amount of contribution (see Grades).
  • Grade 5 adds VIP residents to the daily board, worth much more EXP.

What you get

  • Cheaper shops (5% → 25% off) and storage (95 → 50 mesos).
  • Civic shop items, a resident's chair and 100% scrolls unlocked by grade.
  • The town's earrings at grade 10.

Where the numbers come from

  • Game files (launch, Oct 6): quests, EXP, mesos, contribution per quest and item rewards.
  • OSMS Data Explorer (launch client): contribution needed for each grade.
  • meowdb's Citizenship page: grade levels, discounts, storage fees, how to join, shop lists.
  • meowdb says its info is from the second closed test. Housing, daily contribution limits and reactivation fees aren't confirmed yet.

Grades

Contribution is the total you need for that grade. Daily and weekly amounts come from the game files; contribution comes from the launch client (OSMS formulas page); level gates (unchanged at launch per OSMS) and discounts from meowdb's COT2 notes.
