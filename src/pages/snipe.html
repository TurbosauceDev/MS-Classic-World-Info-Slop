How the FM Sniper works

What it compares

  • Listed now: the cheapest live Free Market listing of each item on meso.watch (one per item; equips, stars and items sold by the set are left out, since their value depends on rolls or set size).
  • Usual price: the median player-shop sale on meso.watch (sales up to ), and the range half of all sales landed in. Compare with can switch to the low end of that range (the 25th percentile) for a stricter test.
  • % of value = listed price ÷ usual price. 40% means it's listed at under half what it usually sells for. Mesos under is the gap per item; for a stack, the whole stack's gap shows under it. Fees, if any, aren't taken off.
  • Over 100% isn't a deal. When meso.watch's trend for the item is up, it's marked rising: the usual price may be catching up with demand.

How much to trust it

  • Sales seen is how many sales the median comes from. Under 5 (meso.watch's "low data") is marked rough: one odd sale can make anything look cheap.
  • Deal labels and Best deal sort are our own cut-offs, not meso.watch's: Steal at 50% or less, Great to 70%, Good to 85%, Small under 100%. Best deal = how far under × trust, where trust grows with sales seen (full at 100+) and is halved for rough prices.
  • Way under (below 25%) or way over (over 3×): often a typo, a bait price or the wrong item. Check in game.
  • Listings update about every 30 minutes; shops open and close fast, so a deal may be gone. The website copy checks for a newer list on its own while this page is open.
  • ☆ Watch items you want (saved in this browser). Watched only shows them in any view, and when a newer list arrives the page names any watched item now under its usual price. New = a listing that wasn't in the list you saw here last time.
