How the FM Sniper works

What it compares

  • Listed now: the cheapest live Free Market listing of each item on meso.watch (one per item; equips, stars and items sold by the set are left out, since their value depends on rolls or set size).
  • Usual price: the median player-shop sale on meso.watch (sales up to ), and the range half of all sales landed in. Compare with can switch to the low end of that range (the 25th percentile) for a stricter test.
  • % of value = listed price ÷ usual price. 40% means it's listed at under half what it usually sells for. Mesos under is the gap per item; for a stack, the whole stack's gap shows under it. Fees, if any, aren't taken off.
  • Under NPC price: listed for less than an NPC pays for it (game files), so buying and vendoring it is profit even with no player sales. Shop sells it for = an NPC shop (meowdb shop list) is cheaper than this listing.
  • Over 100% isn't a deal. When meso.watch's trend for the item is up, it's marked rising: the usual price may be catching up with demand.

How much to trust it

  • Sales seen is how many sales the median comes from. Under 5 (meso.watch's "low data") is marked rough. Volatile = the middle half of sales spans more than 60% of the usual price (our cut-off): the "usual" is shaky.
  • Deal labels, Best deal and Best flip are this site's own cut-offs, not meso.watch's: Steal at 50% or less, Great to 70%, Good to 85%, Small under 100%. Best deal = how far under × trust (grows with sales seen, full at 100+, halved for rough). Best flip = mesos under × sell-through (the share of units put up for sale that sold), so things people actually buy come first.
  • Way under (below 25%) or way over (over 3×): often a typo, a bait price or the wrong item. Check in game.
  • Listings update about every 30 minutes; shops open and close fast. On the list since and Deal lifetimes come from every past listing snapshot, so they're only as fine as the gaps between them.
  • ☆ Watch items (saved in this browser), give them a target price on Watch list, and turn on alerts there. New = a listing that wasn't in the list you saw here last time. Keys on the list: j/k move, w watch, c copy, Enter open the item.
