About the KPQ timer
  • A stopwatch for Kerning PQ rounds, split by stage: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, Last (King Slime), then the Bonus stage. Press Space as each stage ends. Each stage shows how it compares with your best.
  • Clear = 1st stage to leaving the Last stage, the same time the KPQ leaderboard ranks by. The Bonus stage doesn't count toward it.
  • Share game window (optional, desktop browser): the page reads the in-game "Time Left" clock, so a new round starts by itself when its clock appears and the times follow the game's clock. The picture stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Stage changes are still your key press: the page can't see which map you're on.
  • Rounds are saved in this browser only. Turn on Send cleared rounds to put them on the KPQ leaderboard with your character name; they're marked as timed on the site. The Grind Tracker app does all of this by itself, stages and EXP included.
  • Keys: Space start / next stage, Enter end the round here, Backspace undo the last split, Esc throw the round away.
