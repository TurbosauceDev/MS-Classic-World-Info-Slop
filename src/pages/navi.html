How to use the map navigator
  • Type a destination, or open a map's card (click any map name on the site) and press Route here.
  • Pick where you're starting: type a map or tap a town.
  • The route is the quickest one by our travel estimate (30 s per map walked, 45 s per cab ride between Lith Harbor, Henesys, Ellinia, Kerning City, Perion and Sleepywood), the same model the Path Planner uses. Tick No taxi to walk the whole way (fewest maps).
  • Each step says which numbered exit to take; open Minimap to see where it is (highlighted). Dashed numbers are hidden portals.
  • Portals come from the launch game files. Maps you only reach through an NPC (jump quests, KPQ, job tests) have no route.
