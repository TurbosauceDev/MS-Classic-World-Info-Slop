Oct 21, 2026 · KPQ timer and a better KPQ leaderboard

new

New KPQ timer page (Grind Tracker): time Kerning PQ rounds stage by stage with the space bar, see each stage against your best, sum of best and averages, and keep your rounds in this browser. Share your game window and it reads the in-game KPQ clock, so rounds start by themselves and follow the game's time. Turn on sending to put cleared rounds on the leaderboard with your name.

The KPQ leaderboard gets filters (Rush or Bonus, party size, job, period, timed by), each round's stage times on a click, a Fastest stages card, an About box, and your own line highlighted. The server now drops repeats of the same round and keeps where each round was timed. Fixed for the Grind Tracker's next release: the KPQ clock never read seconds ending in 6 or 9.

Oct 21, 2026 · Kill and accuracy breakpoints

Where to train: every map now has a kill badge under its monsters, e.g. 3-hit sure · 1-hit 51%: the hits that always kill its most common monster, and the fastest kill that happens at least half the time. Click it for each monster on the map and the +W.Atk (or Magic ATT) or +main stat that makes one fewer hit a sure kill. The Hit column shows the +Accuracy to hit everything there 100%, and the details list +Acc for 95% and 100% per monster. Uses your damage range (Character Builder hand-off, typed Magic and INT, or the default build) after the monster's defense, level penalty and element; an estimate that assumes each hit rolls evenly between min and max. No badge when there is no real damage range (the 20 × level guess has none); a typed Attack range alone shows hits but not +W.Atk, since its stat split is unknown.

Oct 20, 2026 · Launch-client formulas

update

Damage, defense, EXP and meso numbers now follow the launch game client instead of the second closed test, as read by the OSMS Data Explorer formulas page. Mastery counts ×0.7 (was ×0.8), so minimum hits are a bit lower. Magic damage uses INT/125 (was /100), about 10% less at 100 INT. Damage taken (Danger) uses the new defense formula with flat DEF/25 and level/10 cuts. Iron Arrow loses 20% per extra target, compounding (1, 0.8, 0.64, 0.512). EXP for levels 90-99 is 2.1× the level before (Lv 90 needs 11.9M), and the whole table counts as confirmed now. Mesos per kill are floor(2.1 × monster level) + 3, ±10%, on about 67% of kills (fitted to in-game captures by azzy), roughly 30% less than the old estimate. Party EXP bonus adds 5% for each extra member who hits the monster (4 players: 45%, was 30%). Crafting stops at Lv 8, and Citizenship grades need 1,500 to 32,000 contribution.
