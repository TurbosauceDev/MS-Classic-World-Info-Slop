About this guide
  • Kerning City Party Quest (KPQ, "First Time Together"): 4 players, level 21+, 5 stages ending with King Slime, then a bonus room.
  • Maps, monster stats and spots, the quest and items come from the launch game files. Hover or click any monster, item or map name for its card.
  • Puzzle answers, Cloto's questions, box contents and tips come from meowdb's KPQ guide; the game files don't hold them (the server runs the PQ).
  • Minimaps: coloured dots = monster spawn spots; yellow C / N tags show where Cloto and Nella stand.
