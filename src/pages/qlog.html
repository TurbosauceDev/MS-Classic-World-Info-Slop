How the Quest log works
  • Press Read my screen and share the game window. Open the in-game QUEST window on the Completed tab, open one category, scroll slowly to its end, close it, open the next. Then do the same on In Progress. What's read shows as you go; press Stop reading when done.
  • No screen share? Record the same thing (Windows: Win+Alt+R, or OBS) and drop the video, or paste screenshots of the window.
  • Undo / Redo (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y) take back a read or a mark; Forget what was read starts over.
  • The site reads each row's name against the quests that category can hold, in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Questlines show under their line's name (like "Icarus's Dream"), so one row can mean several quests.
  • The pills say how many quests were read per category against the number on the game's bar. Fix anything missed with Done / On it on a row.
  • Can accept now comes from the game files: quests you haven't done, at or under your level, for your class (your Path Planner class), whose earlier quests are done. The Available tab isn't needed.
  • Done quests are shared with Keep or sell ("skip quests I've done") and the Path Planner.
  • Plan: one route for every quest you tick, plus the earlier quests they need. Each monster is hunted once for every quest that wants it (kill counts count for all accepted quests at once, drops add up), shop items are bought on the way, and the stops are ordered for the least travel and kill time. Times are estimates from your Path Planner character; item drops use its assumed drop rate.
