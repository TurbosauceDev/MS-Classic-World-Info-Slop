What Movers shows and where the numbers come from

What a row is

  • The biggest day-over-day price changes across the markets we read, one row per item and market that has a yesterday price and a today price.
  • meso.watch: today's median sale price vs yesterday's, from their published 7-day daily medians; the sparkline is the same history. Low-data items are left out.
  • Change compares each market against itself, never one site against another, so the semantic differences between sites (asks vs sales) don't pollute the row.

Reading it

  • Rising rows are items getting more expensive, Falling cheaper. Dimmed sales counts rest on fewer than 5 sales.
  • Sales counts differ per market: meso.watch counts that day's sold slots.
  • Equips are priced by roll, so a big move can be one scrolled item. Check the item page before trading.
  • Click an item to open its page: all sources side by side, where to buy, history.
