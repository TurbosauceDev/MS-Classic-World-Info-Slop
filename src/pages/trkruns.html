About shared runs
  • Real runs from tracker players with sharing on, not our model. Each is 15 minutes of non-stop farming on one map.
  • One player per home network, one line per party code, so a household or party can't count as many players.
  • Until 3 or more players have run a map, its numbers are marked early. The same data shows as "players:" lines in Where to train and as Confirmed Drops on monster and item pages.
