How to read this table

Columns

  • Bonus: how much the quest multiplies the EXP of the kills it asks for. 3× means the quest triples what those kills were worth.
  • Equals: the quest's EXP as kills of its target monster. If the items take fewer kills than that, the quest beats grinding it.
  • Questline: quests that follow on from each other share a questline name, the one your in-game quest log shows while you are on it (like "Icarus's Dream"). Click it to see the whole line in order; searching the name finds its quests too.
  • Hover a quest name for its story, requirements and full rewards, or a reward icon for the item's stats.
  • Must do / Recommended / Worth doing / Situational / Skip: what the community guides say about the reward (metaroad.gg, maplestory.quest, meowdb), with the reason under the name. "Valuable quests only" shows only those.

What's included

  • Every quest you can do at launch, except Citizenship quests (they have their own tab).
  • Orbis, El Nath and Forgotten Hollow quests are hidden until those areas open.
  • Item-only quests show no Bonus, since drop rates aren't known yet.
