How to use the skill database
  • Every 1st and 2nd job skill in the launch game files, for all 14 jobs. 3rd job isn't in the launch, so it's left out.
  • Search by skill name, job or anything in its description or effects (e.g. "speed", "MP -", "Def."). Filter by class, job, 1st/2nd job and type. Reset filters clears everything. Click a column to sort.
  • Click a skill to open its page: description, the effect at every level, which skill it needs first and which skills it unlocks.
  • Names, icons, max levels, requirements and effects come straight from the game files. Type is our own label from the game files' skill kind (Debuff = lowers a monster's stats without dealing damage).
