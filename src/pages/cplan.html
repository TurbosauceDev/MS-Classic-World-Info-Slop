How the Craft planner works
  • Set your craft level in each profession (0 = not learned; crafting caps at Lv at launch) and add your inventory: the same Equip, Use, Etc and bank screenshots the Price check reads (drop them on either page, both see them), or type items in by hand.
  • Craft now: recipes your levels allow and your materials cover, with how many times you could make each on its own. Parts you can craft yourself (ore into ingots, say) are made on the way. Profit = what the result sells for (median player-shop sale on meso.watch, as of , or what an NPC pays if that's more) minus the craft fee (game files) minus what your materials would sell for. Switch My materials cost to "nothing" to count them as free.
  • Almost: recipes where you have some of the materials. Each missing item shows the cheapest way to buy it (live listing, NPC shop or usual price) and the best maps to farm it with an estimated time.
  • Farming times are estimates. No drop rates exist in the game files or anywhere else, so every reported drop (meowdb player reports, or a monster the item is named after) is taken as 5% per kill (this site's assumption). Where grind tracker players shared 100+ kills of a monster, their real drop count is used instead. Kills per hour come from Where to train's model: your class and level with the default build, killing alone.
  • From your inventory: the best mix of crafts using only what you hold, best profit per craft first. Equips are valued as plain ones (median sale); good rolls sell for more, bad ones for less. Crafting success rates aren't in the files, so every craft is taken as a success.
