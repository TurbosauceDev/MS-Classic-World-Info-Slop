About the Grind Tracker
  • Our own Windows app for MapleStory Classic World. It sits on top of the game while you grind and does the counting for you.
  • What players share shows up in Shared runs and the KPQ leaderboard, and as "players:" lines and Confirmed Drops elsewhere on the site.

MS Classic Grind Tracker

Windows · free
  • EXP/hr and time to level from the EXP bar, plus start and end readings.
  • Kills, mesos/hr and loot from the pickup lines, each item with its NPC and player-shop value.
  • Potions and ammo used and what they cost, read off your quick slots and Use tab.
  • Deaths confirmed from the chat line, with the EXP lost.
  • Party view: share a party code to see everyone's EXP/hr live.
  • KPQ mode: stage and round times, best and average round, and your clears on the KPQ leaderboard.
  • History of every session, with CSV export and side-by-side compare.
Download for Windows

Windows 10/11, about 110 MB. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may warn: More info → Run anyway. Your browser may also ask to keep the file. It updates itself after that.

Getting started

  1. Install and open it, then pick the game window when asked.
  2. Press Start when you begin grinding and Stop when you're done.
  3. Open your Use tab once so it can see your potions.
  4. Say yes to sharing if you want your runs to count in Shared runs and the KPQ leaderboard.
  5. More in How to use, or press ? or F1 in the app.

Screen-read only

It only reads the game's picture, the same screen capture OBS and Discord use. It never touches game files or memory and never presses keys or clicks.

Sharing is your choice

Off until you say yes on first start. Shared runs carry map, level, job, party size, EXP, kills and drops. Never your character name.

Updates itself

New versions download in the background and install when you close it. Settings › Check for updates checks right away.
