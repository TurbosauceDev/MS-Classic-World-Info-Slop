How the Market Comparison Index works

What each column is

  • One column per price site we pull, each showing that site's own typical sale price: meso.watch median sale, mesoindex.com usual price, mesoarbitrage.com median sale (shown 2 h late), classicmarket.kr combined market median, meowdb usual price (mixes asks seen in shops with sales, so it runs high on cheap loot).
  • A site with only asks and no sales for an item is left blank here. The item's own page has every row in detail.
  • Middle = median of the sites shown. Spread = most expensive site ÷ cheapest site.
  • Cheapest live = the cheapest listing seen in the last day on meso.watch, mesoindex or meowdb. Shops close fast; cheap ones are often gone. Equips are any roll, so no "% under" for them.

Reading it

  • Green = cheapest site for that item, amber = most expensive. Dimmed cells rest on fewer than 5 sales.
  • Big spreads usually mean one site has few sales or counts asks, not a real price gap. Check the sales count (hover a cell).
  • Click an item to open its page: all prices, where to buy, history.
  • classicrank.com is rankings only.
