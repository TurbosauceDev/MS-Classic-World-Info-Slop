About the weekly ladder
  • EXP and mesos per hour from Grind Tracker players with sharing on, ranked over one week (Monday 00:00 UTC, reset every Monday). One line per player (home network) or party (same party code).
  • The name on a line is the display name that player chose in the tracker's Settings (opt-in — the field is empty by default). No name shows as an unnamed grinder.
  • A board line ranks the best run of the week and also shows the week's total farmed time; a line needs 30 farmed minutes to appear.
  • Watchdogs: a run far above what a map's spawn points can physically give, or far above the rest of a busy board, is held back — the count under the board says how many. Held-back runs stay in the owner's inbox; nothing is deleted and no one is named.
  • Map rates: what the community actually measures on a map this week — the median over players of each player's median EXP/h there. A map shows once 2+ players farmed it this week.
