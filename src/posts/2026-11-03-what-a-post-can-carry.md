The second sample post: a tour of what a news post on this feed can include, written the way a real one will arrive. The pipeline behind it is the simple one - write the draft somewhere comfortable, a Google Doc or a Discord message, and an agent files it here as a post exactly like this one. No formatting work, no code, and the next build ships it.

### News, as it happens

When an official announcement drops, a scheduled job can turn it into a post here the same hour. Official wording goes in a quote, our own notes stay outside it:

> The server will undergo a maintenance check on Thursday, November 5, from 1:00 AM to 3:00 AM UTC. Characters will be unable to log in during the window.

**Bold** flags the parts that matter, *italics* add a quieter remark, and a precise detail like `1:00 AM UTC` can be code style so it survives copy-paste exactly.

- Highlights go in a list, one line each
- A [link to the source](https://maplestory.nexon.net/) rides along with the summary
- Game names link themselves, no markup needed: the road to **Mushmom** or a pair of **Work Gloves** is one click away
- Hover a linked name for its game-data tooltip, click through to its page, and the browser's back button - or the site's - drops you back in the post exactly where you left it

### What lands on this feed next

Journal notes from the team, official-news reposts from a watcher job, and patch breakdowns straight from the draft doc. Nothing about the pipeline changes how a post reads: one file, one commit, and the feed above updates.
