    Ctrl+K opens this from anywhere in the window. Enter runs the pick, arrows move, Esc closes. For shortcuts that work while the game has focus, see Settings > Keyboard shortcuts.

    Help

    Getting started
    • Open MapleStory, then press Start. The tracker finds the game window by itself.
    • Keep the EXP bar, the minimap and the chat box on screen. Kills, mesos and drops are read from the chat lines; level and EXP from the EXP bar; the map from the minimap title.
    • For the first few seconds the self-check shows what it can read. A ✗ comes with what to fix and turns ✓ by itself once it works.
    • Press Stop when you're done: the timer stops and the tracker asks for one last look at your inventory. Press Finalize log to save it to Past sessions and see the run summary (a name, typed under Start, and a note are optional). Resume carries on instead.
    What the numbers mean
    • EXP/hr: EXP per hour of farming. Breaks over a minute (no EXP, no pickups) are left out. "Last 5 min" is your speed right now.
    • Next level in: time to level at the current EXP/hr, with your level and EXP %.
    • Mesos/hr: mesos picked up per hour. "Net" takes off the potions and arrows you used.
    • Kills/hr: from the "You received EXP" lines. When lines scroll by too fast, an estimate of the real number is shown under it.
    • Loot value/hr: drops at player-shop prices (meso.watch), or the NPC price when there's none.
    • Deaths: the EXP bar dropping without a level-up, checked against the "You have lost ... EXP" chat line.
    Modules and layout
    • ▦ Modules on the title bar (or View → Modules toolbox, Ctrl+Shift+K) opens the toolbox: drag a module from it into your layout, or press + to add it at the bottom. Press × on a box, or drag it back onto the toolbox, to put it away.
    • Every box can be moved by its ⠿ grip (onto another box) and folded with –. Drop it on the left or right third of another box to put the two side by side (the dashed preview shows where it lands). Hold Shift (or Alt) to show the resize bars: drag the one between two boxes to share the width, or the one under a box to make it taller or shorter. Double-click a bar to undo it. With a side-by-side bar focused, the left and right arrow keys move it too. Settings → Reset box layout puts them back.
    • Run log: what you gained every 2 seconds (EXP and kills, mesos, loot), newest at the bottom. Scroll up to read back; it stays put until you scroll down again.
    • Event log: everything else that happened, with the time: map changes, level ups, deaths, party and job, pauses, KPQ stages and rounds, 15-minute run results, and every message the tracker shows you (the line under Start is only the newest one). Read with the run log, it tells the story of the run.
    • Snapshots: EXP/hr from only two readings of the EXP bar (start and now), like mapleexp.com. A good check on the live number. Type a level or % to fix one that read wrong.
    • Past sessions (F2, or File > Past sessions): your last 7 days, how long each level took, Maps (EXP/hr per map), your best runs, search and filters, details per run (monsters, loot, potions used, EXP by map), Compare (tick 2 to 6 runs), and Export CSV. Edit run in a run's details fixes its name, note, map, farming time, EXP, mesos, kills or deaths when something read wrong; the first values stay listed under it.
    • A run's map is the one you got the most EXP on, not where you stood at Stop: dying, a town scroll or walking to a shop doesn't make that town your training map.
    • Esc opens Settings (and closes any pop-up). Reset, next to Start when you're not tracking (also File > Reset tracker), clears the numbers, logs and last summary for a fresh start; saved runs stay in Past sessions.
    • Settings (File → Settings, or Ctrl+,): job, potion box, alerts, goal level, party code, sharing, updates and bug reports.
    Potions or arrows not counted
    • Put them on your quick slots, or open the Use tab of your inventory.
    • Still ✗? Make sure the numbers under the slots aren't covered (by the chat box, a window or the mouse), then send a bug report.
    Playing in a party
    • Everyone types the same party code in Settings. You'll see each other's EXP/hr, level and job, updated every minute. A name only shows if you type one.
    • Open the in-game party window so the tracker knows the party size.
    Inventory and loot
    • Open your inventory on Equip, Use and Etc for a second each after Start, and again before Stop. The tracker reads every slot (picture and count) by itself; ✓✓ on a tab means it was read twice. A slot that misses one read keeps its last count.
    • Loot = what's in Etc (plus equips and scrolls) now minus at the first look, or the pickup lines, whichever is more. That covers lines that didn't read; the lines cover loot you sold in between.
    • Only the slots you can see are read: scroll to the top of a tab.
    • After Start the Inventory line in the main card asks for all 3 tabs until each was read; click a tab there to read it now. After Stop, each tab turns green once it was read again; the run summary shows each item before and after.
    KPQ runs
    • On Auto, KPQ turns on by itself once a KPQ stage shows on the minimap. Pick KPQ in the KPQ box to also skip the wait in Kerning City before the first round.
    • During KPQ the session pauses on any map that isn't a KPQ stage (Kerning City between rounds) and picks back up on the next stage. Each trip into the stages is one round.
    • Bonus or Rush is only the label saved with the session. History shows rounds, and EXP/hr counts time in the stages only.
    • KPQ rounds don't count as farming runs. With sharing on, each round cleared from the 1st stage goes to the KPQ leaderboard on the site instead (clear time, party size, level, job and your character name, unless you turn the name off in Settings).
    Window tips
    • Simple Mode (was Mini) shrinks the window to the run at a glance: map, EXP gained, EXP per minute and your best minute, the level bar since Start, potions used and picked up, mesos spent, picked up and loot value, and an EXP-per-minute chart. Full view brings everything back.
    • Focus Mode is a third view: the same numbers in one centred column with room to breathe, soft shadows and quiet highlights (skinned after maplestory.watch's market page), in your theme's colours. A narrow window hides the button — the View menu or Ctrl+Shift+F still switch, and Full view comes back the same ways.
    • Overlay Mode is a fourth view, and the first one that leaves this window: floating widgets over the game itself, on any monitor. Ctrl+Shift+O shows and hides it; Ctrl+Shift+E (or View → Edit overlay layout) unlocks it for editing — drag the cards anywhere, tick what they track, then Done locks it and every click goes through to the game again. The Map & spawns card shows the map from the minimap title with its spawn table and boss respawn timers. Positions and choices are remembered.
    • The main card has a Per hour / Total switch for its numbers, your Start → now EXP readings (fix lets you type a level or % that read wrong) and the Inventory tabs (green = read, click one to read it now).
    • Loot value: Etc monster drops (caps, teeth, shells) count at the NPC sell price, since players don't buy them; ores, jewels, crafting materials, equipment and scrolls at the player-shop price from meso.watch.
    • The window stays on top of the game. Turn that off in Settings → Window and sharing.
    • Ctrl + and Ctrl - make the text bigger or smaller.
    • Pause (next to Start while tracking) stops the clock for a break; nothing picked up counts until you press Go on.
    Shortcuts and themes
    • Shortcuts work with the game in front: Ctrl+Shift+T keep on top, Ctrl+Shift+S Start/Stop, P Pause, M Simple Mode, F Focus Mode, O Overlay Mode, E edit the overlay's layout, K Modules toolbox (also in View), H hide/show, I read the open inventory tab, B report a bug, X click-through (clicks go to the game under the window; the same keys turn it off), N mark the moment in the run note, G save the numbers as a picture, U mute sounds. Change or turn them off in Settings → Shortcuts and window.
    • Settings → Theme: pick a colourway, or Edit colours and save your own. Copy share code gives a one-line code friends paste under Share a theme; Save as file makes a .mstheme file.
    • Settings → Shortcuts and window → See-through window makes the tracker partly transparent over the game.
    Something reads wrong
    • Ctrl+Shift+B (even with the game in front; changeable in Settings → Shortcuts and window), the Bug button or Settings → Report a bug saves the last ~25 seconds of game pictures and a log to a folder. Your character name shows in those pictures. Send the folder to Danny.
    • File → Open frames and bug reports folder shows where they are.
    Updates
    • Updates download by themselves and install when you close the app.
    • Settings → Check for updates checks now. When one is ready, a banner on top offers Restart and update; it saves your session first.
    Privacy and safety
    • The tracker only looks at the game window's picture, the same way OBS or Discord screen share does. It never reads the game's memory or files and never presses keys or clicks for you.
    • Sharing (your choice, Settings) sends map, level, job, EXP, kills and drops per 15 minutes, never your character name. The name only goes with KPQ rounds, for the KPQ board (Settings can turn that off).

    Settings

    Reading the game

    Party

    Everyone in the party types the same code to see each other's EXP/hr, level and job live (a name only if you type one). The party also counts as one group in the shared numbers.

    Window and sharing

    Layout

    Profiles: your whole setup (where boxes are, their sizes, the window size, the theme and the switches above)
    Boxes move by their ⠿ grip: onto the left or right of another box for side by side, or above or below it. The ▦ Modules toolbox adds and puts away boxes. Hold Shift (or Alt) to resize them; double-click a bar, or a box's title, to fit it again. "Fit every box now" undoes the widths and heights you set but keeps where the boxes are. "Line up every box now" snaps the edges of boxes side by side into shared columns, gives every box in a row the same top and bottom, and keeps the gaps even.

    Shortcuts and window

    Keyboard shortcuts
    These work from anywhere, the game too. Click one and press the new keys (Ctrl, Alt or Shift plus a key, or an F key on its own); Esc cancels, Backspace turns it off. While the tracker runs, a shortcut's keys don't reach other programs, so free any that clash with your game keys. Inside the window, Ctrl+K opens the quick jump — type to reach any module, view or pop-up.
