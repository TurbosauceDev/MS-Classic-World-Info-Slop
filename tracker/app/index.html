Help

Getting started
  • Open MapleStory, then press Start. The tracker finds the game window by itself.
  • Keep the EXP bar, the minimap and the chat box on screen. Kills, mesos and drops are read from the chat lines; level and EXP from the EXP bar; the map from the minimap title.
  • For the first few seconds the self-check shows what it can read. A ✗ comes with what to fix and turns ✓ by itself once it works.
  • Press Stop when you're done: the timer stops and the tracker asks for one last look at your inventory. Press Finalize log to save it to History and see the run summary (a name, typed under Start, and a note are optional). Resume carries on instead.
What the numbers mean
  • EXP/hr: EXP per hour of farming. Breaks over a minute (no EXP, no pickups) are left out. "Last 5 min" is your speed right now.
  • Next level in: time to level at the current EXP/hr, with your level and EXP %.
  • Mesos/hr: mesos picked up per hour. "Net" takes off the potions and arrows you used.
  • Kills/hr: from the "You received EXP" lines. When lines scroll by too fast, an estimate of the real number is shown under it.
  • Loot value/hr: drops at player-shop prices (meso.watch), or the NPC price when there's none.
  • Deaths: the EXP bar dropping without a level-up, checked against the "You have lost ... EXP" chat line.
The tabs
  • Session: farming time, deaths, potion costs, your goal, party, boss respawns and monsters killed.
  • Loot: mesos in and out, items looted and consumables used, each with a total.
  • Every box can be moved by its ⠿ grip (onto another box, onto a tab, or above the tabs to see it on every tab) and folded with –. Settings → Reset box layout puts them back.
  • Snapshots: EXP/hr from just two readings of the EXP bar (start and now), like mapleexp.com. A good check on the live number. Type a level or % to fix one that read wrong.
  • History: past sessions, your last 7 days, how long each level took, and Export CSV.
  • Settings: job, potion box, alerts, goal level, party code, sharing, updates and bug reports.
Potions or arrows not counted
  • Put them on your quick slots, or open the Use tab of your inventory.
  • Still ✗? Make sure the numbers under the slots aren't covered (by the chat box, a window or the mouse), then send a bug report.
Playing in a party
  • Everyone types the same party code in Settings. You'll see each other's EXP/hr, level and job, updated every minute. A name only shows if you type one.
  • Open the in-game party window so the tracker knows the party size.
Inventory and loot
  • Open your inventory on Equip, Use and Etc for a second each after Start, and again before Stop. The tracker reads every slot (picture and count) by itself; ✓✓ on a tab means it was read twice.
  • Loot = what's in Etc (plus equips and scrolls) now minus at the first look, or the pickup lines, whichever is more. That covers lines that didn't read; the lines cover loot you sold in between.
  • Only the slots you can see are read: scroll to the top of a tab.
  • After Start the Session tab asks for all 3 tabs until each was read. After Stop, each tab turns green once it was read again; the run summary shows each item before and after.
KPQ runs
  • On Auto, KPQ turns on by itself once a KPQ stage shows on the minimap. Pick KPQ in the Session tab to also skip the wait in Kerning City before the first round.
  • During KPQ the session pauses on any map that isn't a KPQ stage (Kerning City between rounds) and picks back up on the next stage. Each trip into the stages is one round.
  • Bonus or Rush is just the label saved with the session. History shows rounds, and EXP/hr counts time in the stages only.
  • KPQ sessions aren't sent as shared farming runs.
Window tips
  • Mini shrinks the window to a strip with EXP/hr and time to level. Click the strip to bring it back.
  • The window stays on top of the game. Turn that off in Settings → Window and sharing.
  • Ctrl + and Ctrl - make the text bigger or smaller.
Something reads wrong
  • Ctrl+Shift+B (even with the game in front), the Bug button or Settings → Report a bug saves the last ~25 seconds of game pictures and a log to a folder. Your character name shows in those pictures. Send the folder to Danny.
  • File → Open frames and bug reports folder shows where they are.
Updates
  • Updates download by themselves and install when you close the app.
  • Settings → Check for updates checks now. When one is ready, a banner on top offers Restart and update; it saves your session first.
Privacy and safety
  • The tracker only looks at the game window's picture, the same way OBS or Discord screen share does. It never reads the game's memory or files and never presses keys or clicks for you.
  • Sharing (your choice, Settings) sends map, level, job, EXP, kills and drops per 15 minutes. Never your character name: that is only read for your own History and stays on this PC.
